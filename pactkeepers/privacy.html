Privacy Policy

Effective: 11 October 2026 · Applies to the Pactkeepers app for Android

The short version

Pactkeepers does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your game progress stays on your device.

What the game stores, and where

  • Your save file (your character's name and look, your monsters, items and story progress) is stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. We never receive it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.
  • Settings (sound, text speed and similar) are stored the same way.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers, advertising ID or usage data. The app requests no special Android permissions.

Google Play

The game is distributed through Google Play. When the game checks whether an update is available, that check is handled on your device by the Google Play Store; we do not receive any information from it. If you have allowed it in your device settings, Google may share anonymous crash and performance reports with developers through Google Play Console. Google's own handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Pactkeepers collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts collecting any data, we will update this page before that version is released and change the effective date above.
