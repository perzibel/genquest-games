Monsters, story, music and code

Made for Pactkeepers by GenQuest Games. Monster art was generated on our own computer with the DreamShaper XL model (CreativeML Open RAIL++-M licence) and then selected and edited for the game. The music was composed for the game using instrument samples released into the public domain (CC0): VSCO 2 Community Edition orchestra (Versilian Studios), Virtuosity Drums (Versilian Studios & Karoryfer Samples) and Karoryfer basses.
