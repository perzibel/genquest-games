Privacy Policy

Effective: 17 October 2026 · Applies to the SOC Tycoon app for Android

The short version

SOC Tycoon has no accounts and no analytics of our own. Your game progress stays on your device. The game shows optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

What the game stores, and where

  • Your save file (your company, analysts, clients, tokens and progress) is stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. We never receive it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.
  • Settings (sound, music, reminders) are stored the same way.
  • When you buy a token pack, the game keeps the Google Play order number in your save so the same order is never paid twice. It stays on your device.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Ads appear only when you choose to watch one for a reward. To show them, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and sends to Google: your device's advertising ID, your IP address (used to estimate an approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions, and crash and performance diagnostics. Google uses this for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads using Google's consent form. You can change your choice any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings.

Purchases (Google Play)

Token packs are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. Google's handling of purchase data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders in Settings, the game schedules local notifications on your device (vault full, daily reward ready). They are created on the device; nothing is sent to us.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos or files. We do not sell personal information.

Children

SOC Tycoon is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data use changes, we will update this page before that version is released and change the effective date above.
